Questions

Things you're probably wondering.

What's a "design partner"?
Friendly term for an early customer who helps shape the product. You get full use, free, for 90 days. We get real-world testing and your honest feedback. No commitment past the 90 days from either side.
Will my leads know they're texting AI?
Some will suspect; many won't. AI follow-up is normal in 2026 — your competitors are already doing it. The real question is whether leads convert — and our early data is stronger than templated drips. We'll happily talk through disclosure rules in your state.
What if the AI says something wrong?
Three layers of safety: (1) the AI is tuned to your voice on day one and we adjust it weekly, (2) you get an instant alert before any hot lead progresses, (3) one click pauses the AI for any lead and lets you take over.
Does this replace Follow Up Boss?
No. We sit on top of FUB. You keep using FUB exactly as you do today. Lead intake doesn't change. Conversation activity will appear in FUB once our partner integration completes (in progress).
What about TCPA / opt-outs?
Built in. STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, and similar keywords trigger automatic opt-out — the lead is flagged and we never message them again. Twilio A2P 10DLC compliant.
What happens after the 90 days?
We talk. If it's working, we'll discuss what continuing looks like together. If it isn't, no hard feelings — you've gotten 90 days of free AI follow-up and your data is yours.
